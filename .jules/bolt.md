## 2026-10-06 - Isolating Dynamic Stream Watchers in Flutter Pages **经验心得：** In Flutter Riverpod applications, watching reactive streams (like `inboxTasksProvider`) inside a top-level page widget (`InboxPage`) forces the entire page hierarchy—including heavy static headers, custom typography, and progress bars—to rebuild whenever stream values emit. Extracting the reactive list into a dedicated `ConsumerWidget` isolates rebuild scope strictly to the task list subtree. **后续行动：** When rendering lists bound to Riverpod stream/future providers inside complex pages, encapsulate the provider watcher within a child `ConsumerWidget`.
