## 2025-05-18 - Isolate dynamic stream consumers in Flutter list pages **经验心得：** Listening to dynamic database streams (like `inboxTasksProvider`) at the root or top-level `StatefulWidget` of a page causes heavy parent widgets (headers, progress indicators, control bars) to rebuild on every stream update. Extracting stream watchers into dedicated sub-`ConsumerWidget` components avoids redundant parent widget rebuilds and layout recalculations. **后续行动：** When building Flutter pages with streaming Riverpod providers, isolate dynamic stream subscribers into narrow `ConsumerWidget` leaves.
