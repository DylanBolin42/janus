## 2026-03-30 - Tooltips on Icon Buttons for A11y & Micro-interactions
经验心得： In Flutter, wrapping icon-only action buttons (such as navigation back buttons or modal close/submit actions) with `Tooltip` provides both visual hover/long-press hints and screen reader accessibility labels.
后续行动： Continuously check for icon-only action buttons across newly added or refactored pages and ensure they are wrapped with meaningful `Tooltip`s.
